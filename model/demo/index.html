    What this demonstrates

    Filters choose eligible recipes first. The same supervised ranking weights published with llmmm then order them. Switch to the simple baseline to compare.

    What it does not establish

    This is a separately sourced sample, not the full training catalog or a new quality benchmark. Ingredient frequencies come from this small catalog. Scores are not probabilities of a good meal.

    Search runs in your browser. The app does not send your pantry inputs to a server or store them between visits.
